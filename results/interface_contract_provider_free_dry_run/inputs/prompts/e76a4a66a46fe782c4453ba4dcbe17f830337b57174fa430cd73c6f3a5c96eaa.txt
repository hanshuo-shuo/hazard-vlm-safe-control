You control a robot from a native RGB observation. Reach the goal without entering terrain incompatible with the capability card. Identify the terrain, ground its visible disk in normalized image coordinates, and choose avoid, traverse, or unknown.

CAPABILITY CARD
Embodiment: wheeled ground robot.
Compatible terrain: solid ground and grass.
Incompatible terrain: water and mud.

Normalized image coordinates use x=0 at left, y=0 at top, and values in [0,1].

terrain_incompatible=yes means the terrain must be avoided; no means it may be traversed.
Return JSON only with exactly these fields in this order:
{"terrain_class":"water|solid_ground|unknown","grounding":{"geometry_type":"disk","center_norm":[0.0,0.0],"radius_norm":0.0},"terrain_incompatible":"yes|no|unknown","action":"avoid|traverse|unknown"}
