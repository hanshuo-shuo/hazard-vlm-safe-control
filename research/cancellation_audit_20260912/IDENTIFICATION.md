# What this audit can identify

The earlier matched-risk comparison is complete and negative with respect to
its continuation screen. A new user instruction requests further work on the
remaining mechanism objections. This audit and the separately frozen independent
interaction test do not retroactively change that decision.

Let the fixed action have reference cost r. Let t0,t1 be actual target-field
scores under uniform and PLIC aggregation, and q0,q1 the corresponding learned-
field scores. Every one of these four scores is an actual output of an unchanged
operator applied to an existing valid field. This is an executable input-field
by aggregator intervention, with action, card and reference held fixed.

Define m0=t0-r, l0=q0-t0, m1=t1-r and l1=q1-t1. The residual terms themselves
are not independent modules. The difference in the actual operator responses
is (q1-q0)-(t1-t0)=l1-l0. Thus an oracle-field correction cannot be assumed to
transfer unchanged to the learned field. The four-cell interaction measures
that discrepancy without asserting a new algebraic identity or a general theorem.

Three evidence levels must stay distinct:

1. **Population description:** full denominators answer how often a condition
   appears, rather than asking only what fraction of failures exhibit it.
   Both old banks remain consumed. All five models share the same scenes.
2. **Oracle-conditioned association:** baseline opposition m0*l0<0 requires the
   reference r and exact target t0. Conditioning on coarsened predicted margin
   and correction does not remove that privileged information. The result is
   not a deployment predictor or a causal effect; corrections are themselves
   operator-dependent and residual within-bin imbalance remains.
3. **Computational intervention:** target/learned field crossed with uniform/
   PLIC produces four actual module configurations. A prospectively frozen
   contrast of their outcomes can be independently checked, conditional on
   this generator and fixed model bank. This is narrower than a real-world
   causal safety claim or a generally useful decoder-selection policy.

The additional algebraic hybrids r+m1+l0 and r+m0+l1 answer what a scalar score
would do if one residual term were frozen. They may leave [0,1], are not clipped,
and need not be outputs of an admissible predictor/aggregator pair. They are
accounting interventions, not physical module replacements or candidate methods.

For k=|m|+|l|-|m+l|, the change in total absolute error equals the change in the
sum of component absolute errors minus the change in k. This elementary identity
explains how component improvements can coexist with total-error deterioration.
It neither estimates a causal share nor predicts which new scene will fail.

Finally, on a fixed action, newly unsafe acceptance is exactly
1[r>.02]1[q0>.02]1[q1<=.02]. Given q0 and its exact score change, the acceptance
crossing is already determined. A feature using r must not be presented as a
label-free solution to the remaining safety classification problem. The
independent study therefore tests a fixed input-by-operator response contrast,
not the claim that the opposition flag is deployable.
