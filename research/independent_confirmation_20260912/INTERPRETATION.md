# What the prospective follow-up supports

The original-versus-balanced comparison is the predeclared primary test. It
supports transfer of the earlier data repair to anonymous paths and random
positions under two new procedural appearance families. The crossed seed/scene
interval for regret reduction excludes zero, and the false-safe guard passes.
This is stronger evidence than repeating the consumed pilot probes, while still
being an internally designed synthetic component experiment rather than a
formal C3-Safe policy evaluation.

The independent-geometry recipe performs better on the random tests, but its
comparison is secondary. It changes training **and** validation distributions,
patch positions, sizes, rotations, shapes, and the source raster from 48x48 to
64x64. All models still receive 64x64 RGB and predict 16x16 fields. Consequently,
the comparison estimates this combined data/rendering change; it does not
isolate the causal effect of property/location independence or exclude an
effect from target rasterization. A matched-resolution ablation on a new frozen
split is needed for that narrower claim. Shared early-stopping rules also
produce different realized epoch counts in two runs.

The data repair is useful but cost calibration remains unresolved. On the new
appearance test, independent training reaches 97.77% paired correctness and
0.0003475 A/C/D regret, while 9.85% of dangerous candidate entries are called
safe. Raw acceptance at the fixed 0.02 threshold is 83.86%, with 9.32% unsafe
decisions among those accepted. The pair-block residual calibration offsets
are all larger than 0.02, producing zero acceptance at that threshold for every
recipe. This is zero coverage and undefined conditional risk, not a usable
zero-risk policy. Other threshold-sweep points are descriptive and do not
authorize changing the fixed safety budget after looking at test performance.

Better perception also produces narrower IID-calibrated upper costs, whose
failure rate is 20.60% on new appearance for the independent recipe. The
calibration procedure has no asserted coverage guarantee under that shift.
The held-out IID pair failure rate is 5.97% for the same recipe; a fixed
calibration set need not yield exactly the nominal 5% empirical failure rate.
These costs are a synthetic mean-exposure convention, not physical collision
probabilities or C3-Safe's q95 interface.

The new relation-head result qualifies the pilot's exact-regret equality.
With poor original perception, frozen no-CF improves new-appearance regret by
0.002538 relative to the same-perception formula. With balanced perception it
worsens regret by 0.000743, and with independent perception by 0.0000182. CF's
difference from the formula with independent perception is only about
0.000000202, in the direction of slightly greater regret. Numerical rounding
in the six-decimal paper table does not imply exact equality. These findings
do not establish that learned relations never help, or that their improvement
with poor perception constitutes genuine semantic reasoning. That mechanism
has not been isolated. The analytic formula remains a necessary control.

The next study should separate the inherited minus-two logit shift, target
rasterization, boundary errors, and the conservatism of simultaneously bounding
all cards/actions in a scene pair. It should keep the current calibrated and
uncalibrated arms, freeze a new calibration/validation policy and test split,
and judge both unsafe decisions and nonzero acceptance. Current test scenes
are consumed. The next teacher stage needs recognizable terrain depictions and
human semantic reference labels; these symbolic textures alone do not qualify
a VLM teacher. Closed-loop comparisons follow after the component can meet
its stated acceptance/safety objective.
